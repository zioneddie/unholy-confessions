Privacy

We don't ask for your name, email, or phone number. Your confession is sent privately to one person, the Keeper, who reads it and may post it on X or TikTok. Don't include names or details that could identify you or anyone else.

What I won't post

I read every confession myself. I don't post names, phone numbers, or accusations against real people, and I may skip or lightly edit anything that could expose someone.

If it's more than a confession

About

I built this because I was carrying something I couldn't say out loud. Not dangerous. Just heavy.

It made me wonder what everyone else is holding that they never say.

So here's a place to put it down. No name, no account, no one you'll ever have to face. I read every confession myself, and I post what I can on X and TikTok, lightly edited only to remove anything identifying.

It might be petty. It might be strange. It might be the thing you've never told anyone. It all belongs here, and someone out there is carrying the same thing and will feel a little less alone reading yours.
