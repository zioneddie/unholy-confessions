About

I built this because I was carrying something I couldn't say out loud. Not dangerous. Just heavy.

It made me wonder what everyone else is holding that they never say.

So here's a place to put it down. No name, no account, no one you'll ever have to face. I read every confession myself, and I post what I can on X and TikTok, lightly edited only to remove anything identifying.

It might be petty. It might be strange. It might be the thing you've never told anyone. It all belongs here, and someone out there is carrying the same thing and will feel a little less alone reading yours.

The door is open.

The Keeper

Privacy

You must be 18 or older to submit. We don’t ask for your name, email, or phone number. Your confession goes privately to one person (the Keeper). Please remove all names and any details that could identify you or anyone else.

What I won't post

I read every confession myself. I don't post names, phone numbers, or accusations against real people, and I may skip or lightly edit anything that could expose someone.

Terms

  • 18+ only.
  • Don't submit anything involving minors, sexual content about real people, or accusations or details about real people.
  • By submitting, you allow the Keeper to read, lightly edit and post your confession anonymously on X and TikTok, or choose not to post it.
  • Not every confession is posted.
  • If a confession suggests someone is in danger, it may not be posted, and help resources are listed in the Need help section.
  • This site is not a counselling or emergency service.

Take-down request

Regret sending something, or see yourself in a post? DM @theunholykeeper on X and quote the confession text (or send the link). I’ll remove it from my accounts as soon as I can.
