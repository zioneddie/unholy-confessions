Before You Confess

Your trust matters. Please understand how submissions are handled.

  • Your confession is sent to the site's administrator through Telegram.
  • Submissions may be reviewed for possible publication; sending one does not mean it will be published.
  • Do not include full names, addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other identifying details.
  • The form does not ask for your name, but complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed. Website infrastructure may process technical information.
  • Do not use this form for emergencies or to report an immediate threat.
